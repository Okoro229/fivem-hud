Questions I get before hiring

Do you need access to my server?

Not for a quote. For installation, yes — or I send you a build to install yourself. I never ask for your database, your Tebex account or your hosting panel.

What if it doesn't work on my server?

You get the build before paying. If it fails on your setup and I can't fix it, you owe nothing. That's the whole point of testing before invoicing.

Do you work on an existing server, or only new ones?

Existing. Most of the work is untangling something that already runs — conflicting resources, scripts that loop server-side for data the client already has, and frameworks that were forked from a tutorial.

What frameworks do you actually know?

QBCore, Qbox and ESX. Every free resource here runs on all three without a framework lock-in, which is the quickest way to check the claim.
